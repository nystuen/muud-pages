Privacy policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Muud is a personal mood journal. This policy explains how the current version of the app handles information.

Information you enter

Muud lets you save a mood, date, selected activities, and an optional note. This information is stored in the app’s local database on your device. Muud does not send journal entries to a Muud server. The current version does not provide account-based or iCloud syncing.

Daylio CSV import and export

When you choose a Daylio CSV file, Muud reads the selected file and imports supported dates, mood labels, activities, and notes into the local database. Importing does not upload the file to Muud. Daylio scales and note titles are not represented by Muud.

When you export, Muud creates a CSV file and presents Apple’s file-export interface. You choose where to save or share it. A destination you choose may handle the file under its own privacy policy.

Data sharing and tracking

The current version has no Muud account or server and does not include advertising, analytics, or social-media SDKs. Muud does not sell or share journal entries with the developer or third-party advertising or analytics services.

Muud does not request access to Health data, contacts, location, camera, or microphone. Apple may process information independently when you download the app, use Apple system services, or enable device backups. Those services are governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Entries remain in the app on your device until you delete them or remove the app and its data. You can delete an individual entry from History using its context menu. Muud does not keep a server-side copy. If device backups are enabled in iOS, Apple’s backup settings may affect copies of app data.

Children

Muud is a general personal journaling app and is not designed to collect children’s information. Because the current version does not transmit entries to the developer, the developer does not knowingly receive personal information from children through the app.

Changes to this policy

If Muud’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before or when the change is introduced. The effective date above will also be updated.
